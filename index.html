Planet Planet/BD

Exoplanet criteria

This catalog includes objects with an estimated mass up to about 60 Jupiter masses (M_Jup). Following the criterion used by exoplanet.eu/catalog, this upper bound is based on the mass–density–radius distribution, which shows a clear separation between giant planets and stars around 60 M_Jup (Hatzes & Rauer, 2015). Two other mass limits are commonly used elsewhere in the literature: 13 M_Jup, based on the deuterium-burning limit, and 30 M_Jup, based on planet-formation scenarios.

Objects with masses greater than 30 M_Jup are labeled as being at the Planet/Brown Dwarf (BD) boundary, because the NASA Exoplanet Archive defines planets as objects with masses of 30 M_Jup or less.

Free-floating candidates

Free-floating planet (FFP) candidates are kept as a separate category and excluded from the "Total entries" count and the "Announcements per year" chart. The IAU definition of a planet requires it to orbit a star — an FFP has no bound host star by construction, so strictly speaking it does not meet that definition. These are therefore listed as planet candidates, not confirmed planets; their count is shown separately in the "Free-floating candidates" figure above.
